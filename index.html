PhD Candidate in Management · Bocconi University

I study how founders and managers learn to decide under uncertainty.

An economist by training, I combine field experiments, new measurement and causal machine learning, often in developing and institutionally fragile settings. I am advised by Alfonso Gambardella and Arnaldo Camuffo at the ION Management Science Lab.

I measure how scientifically managers decide, study which founders benefit from learning to decide like scientists, and explore what language models reveal about decisions inside organizations. My research in 5 minutes →

I am always happy to hear from coauthors, startup programs and organizations interested in how people decide.
