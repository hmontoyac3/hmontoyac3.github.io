I study how founders and managers learn to decide under uncertainty, and who gains when they decide like scientists.

An economist by training, I pair questions from strategy and entrepreneurship with field experiments, new measurement and causal machine learning, often in developing and institutionally fragile settings. Advised by Alfonso Gambardella and Arnaldo Camuffo, at the ION Management Science Lab.

Let’s do research together Research

Research agenda

One question runs through my work: how do people learn to decide?

I measure how scientifically managers make decisions, study which founders benefit from learning to decide like scientists, and explore what language models can reveal about decisions inside organizations.

In the room

From the field to the seminar room, and back.

I build the instruments, run them inside firms and startup programs, and bring the results back to the people who use them.
