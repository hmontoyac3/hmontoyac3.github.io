I study how founders and managers learn to decide under uncertainty, and who gains when they decide like scientists.

An economist by training, I pair questions from strategy and entrepreneurship with field experiments, new measurement and causal machine learning, often in developing and institutionally fragile settings. Advised by Alfonso Gambardella and Arnaldo Camuffo, at the ION Management Science Lab.
