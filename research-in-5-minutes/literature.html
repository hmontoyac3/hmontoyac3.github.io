The evidence so far

Deciding like a scientist: what the RCTs show

Nine randomized controlled trials find that training founders to form theories and test them with evidence changes their decisions and, on average, their performance. My paper asks for whom.

  1. Camuffo, Cordova, Gambardella & Spina (2020)A Scientific Approach to Entrepreneurial Decision Making: Evidence from a Randomized Control TrialManagement ScienceThe first RCT: the scientific approach raises founders' revenue.
  2. Camuffo, Gambardella, Messinese, Novelli, Paolucci & Spina (2024)A scientific approach to entrepreneurial decision-making: Large-scale replication and extensionStrategic Management Journal 45(6)Four RCTs replicate the revenue effect and link the approach to termination.
  3. Novelli & Spina (2024)Making business model decisions like scientists: Strategic commitment, uncertainty, and economic performanceStrategic Management Journal 45(13)The effect depends on how developed the business model is.
  4. Coali, Gambardella & Novelli (2024)Scientific decision-making, project selection and longer-term outcomesResearch Policy 53, 105022How scientific decision-making shapes project selection and longer-term outcomes.
  5. Agarwal, Bacco, Camuffo, Coali, Gambardella, Msangi, Sonka, Temu, Waized & Wormald (2025)Does a Theory-of-Value Add Value? Evidence from a Randomized Control Trial with Tanzanian EntrepreneursOrganization Science 36(2)Adding theory to evidence raises performance, through more coordinated changes.
  6. Gambardella & Messinese (2025)Design- and Theory-Based Approaches to Strategic DecisionsOrganization Science 36(4)Three-arm RCT comparing theory-based and design-based decision-making.
  7. Gagliardi & Novelli (2025)Female Entrepreneurs Targeting Women: Strategic Redirection Under Scientific Decision-MakingOrganization Science 36(2)Women founders targeting women pivot more radically and perform better.
  8. Camuffo, Gambardella & Jannace (2025)Theory-Based Reasoning and PerformanceCEPR Discussion Paper 20300The three-arm RCTs my paper builds on: theory plus evidence outperforms evidence alone.
  9. Battaglia, Colombelli, Panelli, Paolucci & Raguseo (2026)Entrepreneurs-as-Scientists and entrepreneurial team formation strategies: A randomized control trial experimentResearch Policy 55(4), 105461The approach also changes how founders build their teams.
