Measuring scientific decision-making in organizations

Journal
Research Policy, research note
Question
Can we measure scientific decision-making with a short survey?
Data
Validated five ways · 3,178 managers · 54 firms · Italy and Brazil

Why a measure

  • RCTs (Camuffo et al.): founders who decide like scientists perform better
  • Inside firms, no scalable way to measure it: each study codes reasoning by hand
  • A short survey scales: any manager, any firm, repeatable

Years of building it

  • Design12 manager interviews · 49 candidate items
  • ReviewExperts in 5 countries · 16-expert pilot
  • Pre-test462 managers, randomized
  • Field3,178 managers · 54 firms · 2023 to 2026

24 items. Thanks to my coauthors and the team.
